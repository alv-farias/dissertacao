The objective of this work is to study the oriented $P3$ convexity and some parameters related to it. First, a review of the basic concepts of convexity in graphs, and of graph theory in general, is presented. Next, a study of the \emph{Spectrum} problem for the interval number is presented, in which its continuity is verified, that is, all integer values between the bounds belong to the spectrum, and some bounds are then calculated for certain classes of graphs. In addition to structural results, efficient algorithms are presented to obtain the interval number and hull number parameters in oriented trees. Finally, this work also presents complexity results for the class of oriented planar graphs, where both the interval number and the hull number parameters are \npcsi.
